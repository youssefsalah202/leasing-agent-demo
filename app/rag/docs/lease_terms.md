# Lease Terms

We offer lease terms of 6, 12, and 15 months. Shorter-term leases under 12 months carry a $150/month short-term lease premium.

A security deposit equal to one month's rent is required at signing; residents with a qualifying credit score may be eligible for a reduced deposit.

Rent is due on the 1st of the month, with a grace period through the 3rd. A late fee of 5% of the monthly rent applies starting the 4th.

Renters insurance with a minimum of $100,000 in liability coverage is required for all residents.
