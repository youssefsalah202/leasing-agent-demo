# Pet Policy

Maple Grove Apartments is pet-friendly. We welcome up to two pets per apartment, with a combined weight limit of 75 lbs.

There is a one-time non-refundable pet fee of $300 per pet, plus a monthly pet rent of $35 per pet.

We do not allow the following breeds due to insurance restrictions: Pit Bulls, Rottweilers, Doberman Pinschers, and Presa Canarios. Cats are welcome with no breed restrictions.

All pets must be spayed/neutered, up to date on vaccinations, and registered with the leasing office within 5 days of move-in.
