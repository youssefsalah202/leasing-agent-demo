# Application & Qualification

To apply, we require a government-issued ID, proof of income (pay stubs or an offer letter covering the last 30 days), and a $55 non-refundable application fee per applicant.

We look for gross monthly income of at least 3x the monthly rent, a credit score of 620 or higher, and no prior evictions in the last 5 years. Applicants who don't meet the income requirement may qualify with a guarantor whose income is at least 5x the rent.

Applications are typically processed within 1-2 business days. Once approved, a signed lease and deposit are due within 48 hours to hold the unit.
